  --- Morse Code Info: --- 

 Dash is three times longer than dot
 when multiple dots and/or dashes 
 -> leave gap of silence equivalent to the length of one dot between each dot/dash 

 Pauses seperating entire letters leave a gap of silence equivalent to three dots/one dash

 Pauses between two letters is 3 units (3 dots)
 Pauses between two words is 7 units (7 dots)
